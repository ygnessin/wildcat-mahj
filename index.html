Wildcat Mahj

A free game for iPhone and iPad

Practice American mahjong against three computer players. As you play, a list of every hand on the card shows how close you are to each one, best match first if you like. It works offline, and it's free, with no accounts, ads or purchases.

What you get

  • A full game against three computer players: the Charleston, calling, jokers and mahjong.
  • A live list of the card's hands, ranked by how many of your tiles fit each one.
  • An original practice card, plus a way to add a card you own.
  • Eight tile styles, three color themes, and light or dark appearance.
