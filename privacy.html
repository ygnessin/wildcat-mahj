Privacy Policy

Effective: 11 November 2026

The short version

Wildcat Mahj collects no data. The app has no network code, so it can't send anything anywhere. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party code. What the app saves stays on your device.

This policy covers the Wildcat Mahj app for iPhone and iPad and this website. Wildcat Mahj is published by Yuval Gnessin, an individual, for fun. In this policy, "we" means the publisher.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves a few things as files in its own storage on your device:

  • the cards you add, make or edit,
  • your win counts and the hands you've hidden,
  • your filters and settings, such as bot speed, tile style and theme,
  • a game in progress, saved when you leave the app, so you can pick it up again.

These files never leave your device through the app. We can't see them. The app has no sync and no iCloud features. If you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on, iOS includes these files in your device backup, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Apple under your own settings.

Adding your own card

The app comes with one original practice card. You can add a card you own in three ways:

  • Type it in with the app's card editor.
  • Import a card file that you pick, for example one you exported from the app on another device.
  • Turn a photo into a card file. The app shows steps you can follow: photograph a card you own, then ask an AI chat app of your choice to turn the photo into a card file, and import that file.

The photo steps happen outside Wildcat Mahj, only if you choose to follow them. The app doesn't take photos, doesn't open your photo library, and sends nothing to the AI chat app. You share your photos with that service yourself, under its own terms and privacy policy, so read those before you use it.

When you export a card, the app saves it as a file wherever you choose. What happens to it after that is up to you.

Clipboard

The app copies text to your clipboard only when you tap Copy instructions. What it copies is the instruction text for the AI chat app, nothing about you.

The app reads your clipboard only when you tap the system Paste button on the import page, to read a card you copied. You can also paste into text fields yourself, as in any app. The app never reads the clipboard on its own.

No tracking, ads, analytics or accounts

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting tools or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party code or SDKs.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Because we collect nothing, we share nothing with anyone and sell nothing.

One note about Apple: if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may give developers anonymous crash reports and usage numbers. That's between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn it off in Settings. The app adds nothing to it.

Children

Wildcat Mahj is suitable for all ages but isn't made specifically for children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything the app saves is on your device, so you're in control of it:

  • Delete one card: go to Menu › Cards, tap ⋯ next to the card, then Delete card. This works for cards you imported or made. The practice card can't be deleted, but you can undo your edits to it: open its editor with Edit and tap Restore the shipped Wildcat 1337 card.
  • Clear win counts: go to Menu › Cards, tap ⋯, then Reset win counts.
  • Delete everything: delete the app from your device. iOS removes all of its files. Copies in older device backups follow your backup settings.

Since the app collects no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side, and no consent to withdraw. If you used an AI chat app to make a card, ask that service about the photos you shared with it.

This website

This website is a few static pages. It has no cookies, no scripts, no analytics, and loads nothing from other sites. Like almost every website, the company that hosts this website may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visited, to run and protect its service. We don't use those logs to identify or track you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting any data, this page will say so before that version is released.
